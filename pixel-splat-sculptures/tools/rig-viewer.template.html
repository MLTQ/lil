A splat sculpture on a skeleton of bones. Move the pointer over it and it looks toward the pointer. Drag sideways to turn it.

Gesture
Intent the body receives 

      

      

    

  

  
The controller turns that small intent into target angles for each bone. Springs do the easing: stiff bones snap to the pose, soft ones lag and overshoot. Jiggle bones are not driven at all; they are dragged along by whatever they are attached to.
